Chapter twoOne wedding, start to finish

Hannah & Rhys, Buttermere, in October rain.

They booked the whole story and the forecast said rain all day. We walked the shore path between showers, kept the portraits under the old oaks, and saved ten minutes for the light that broke over Fleetwith Pike at 17:40.

The gallery arrived six weeks later, in the order the day happened. Their album followed in December.
