Before you ask

When will we see our photographs?

Sneak peeks arrive within 72 hours. The full gallery follows in 4, 6 or 8 weeks depending on the collection. Peak season (June to September) dates are honoured, never stretched.

What if it rains?

It usually does, at some point. Every booking includes a rain plan with covered spots near your venue. We carry clear umbrellas for you to borrow.

Can we elope on a fell top?

Yes. Most summits need no permit for a small ceremony, but a legal marriage happens at a registered venue or register office. We'll explain the two-step approach most couples use.

How do payments work?

30% retainer to book, balance four weeks before. Bank transfer or card. Dates move once, free, within 18 months.
