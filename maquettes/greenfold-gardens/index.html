We plant
Perennials from 9 cm pots: Geum, Nepeta, Salvia nemorosa. Summer bulbs.
Late gaps filled with Dahlia and Cosmos. Turf only with irrigation.
Tulips, alliums, camassia. Container-grown shrubs while the soil is warm.
Bare-root hedging and roses: beech, hawthorn, Rosa ‘Gertrude Jekyll’.
We cut
Grasses and perennials down by mid-March. Dogwoods coppiced.
Chelsea chop on sedum and phlox. Wisteria summer prune.
Hedges reshaped once. Seedheads kept standing for birds.
Fruit trees and roses. Wisteria back to two buds.
We protect
5 cm mulch of peat-free compost on every bed.
Deep water weekly for new planting, never daily sprinkles.
Dahlias lifted after the first frost. Leaf mould started.
Off the lawns when frosted. Tools serviced, plans for spring.

How a garden happens

Five steps, about one year.

Most projects start in late summer, are built over winter and are planted in time for their first spring.

  1. Week 1 · free

    Site visit

    Ninety minutes in your garden. We test the soil pH, note the light, and talk through budget honestly.

    Soil readingBudget range
  2. Weeks 2–4

    Survey & concept

    A measured survey to the centimetre, then two layout options with sections and photographs of the materials.

    Survey drawingTwo concepts
  3. Weeks 5–8

    Planting plan

    Every plant placed at 1:50, with a four-season schedule. You see what flowers, what holds structure and what dies back, month by month.

    1:50 planPlant scheduleFixed build quote
  4. October – March

    Build & plant

    Hard landscaping first, while the ground is workable. Bare-root plants go in over winter, pots in early spring.

    Weekly photo reportSnag list
  5. The first twelve months

    Aftercare

    We replace anything that fails in year one at no charge, as long as we look after it. Then you choose whether we stay.
