The written plan

No drill turns on before you see the bill.

After your examination you leave with an itemised plan: what we found, what we recommend, each fee and the alternatives. NHS patients receive the FP17DC form. Private patients get the same detail, plus finance options.

  • Fees fixed for 6 months from the date on the plan.
  • The NHS option is always listed when one exists.
  • Sign it at home. There is no same-day pressure.
