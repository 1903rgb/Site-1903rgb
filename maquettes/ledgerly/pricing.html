8200 FAQ

Questions people ask on the first call.

What happens if I miss 31 January?

HMRC charges a £100 penalty the day after, even if you owe no tax. After three months, daily penalties of £10 start, up to £900. Interest also runs on any unpaid balance.

On every plan, we aim to file by mid-December, so the date becomes a payment reminder rather than a deadline.

Do I need to register for VAT?

You must register once your VAT-taxable turnover over the last 12 months goes above £90,000. We watch the rolling total for you and warn you at £75,000. Registering voluntarily earlier can make sense if your clients are VAT-registered businesses.

What does Making Tax Digital change for me?

For VAT, nothing new: every VAT return already goes through MTD software. For Income Tax, sole traders and landlords above the thresholds send quarterly updates to HMRC, then a final declaration by 31 January. Our Essentials and Plus plans include those updates.

Can I switch to you mid-year?

Yes. We contact your previous accountant for professional clearance and take over from there. Most switches take two to three weeks, and there's no fee for the handover.

Should I be a sole trader or a limited company?

It depends on your profit, what you take out, and how much admin you'll tolerate. We run both scenarios on your real figures during the discovery call. It is free and takes about ten minutes.

Which software do you use?

Xero, FreeAgent or QuickBooks. If you already use one, we'll keep it. If not, the licence is included in Essentials, Plus and every limited company plan.
