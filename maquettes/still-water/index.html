Six classes. One pace: the breath.

Every class starts lying down and ends lying down. What happens in between depends on the level you pick.

  • Slow Flow

    Long holds, linked to the breath. Warmth without the rush, and time to find the pose before moving on.

    75min Levels 1–2

  • Vinyasa

    Stronger sequences, arm balances offered, never required. Expect to sweat a little, then lie very still.

    60min Levels 2–3

  • Yin

    Floor-based shapes held for three to five minutes. Bolsters, blankets and the lights turned low.

    75min Open level

  • Breathwork

    Coherent breathing at six breaths a minute, box breath, then a 25-minute guided session lying down. No poses at all.

    60min Open level

  • Foundations

    A four-week course for new students: alignment, props, and the names of things, so other classes stop feeling foreign.

    60min Level 1

  • Restore & Sound

    Supported poses and singing bowls on Sunday evenings. The class people book a week ahead.

    75min Open level

Your first class, minute by minute.

Nerves are normal. Here is exactly what happens, so the only surprise is how quickly the hour goes.

  1. −10

    Arrive

    Shoes off at the door, phone in a locker. Tell the teacher about any injury or pregnancy.

  2. 0:00

    Settle

    Five minutes on your back, breathing in for four and out for six. Same as the ring above.

  3. 0:10

    Move

    A sequence paced to the breath. Options for each level are shown, not just described.

  4. 0:50

    Rest

    Savasana under a blanket. Stay as long as you like afterwards. Nobody rushes you out.
