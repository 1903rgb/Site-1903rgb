Brew day, Tuesday.

Every batch of Viaduct follows the same clock. Here is the one that ends up in your can nine days later.

  1. 06:30

    Mash in

    Maris Otter and a little wheat, rested in hot liquor.

    66 °C · 60 min
  2. 07:40

    Sparge

    Rinse the grain bed, collect the sweet wort.

    76 °C · 20 hl
  3. 08:30

    Boil

    Bittering hops go in at the start of the boil.

    100 °C · 75 min
  4. 10:00

    Whirlpool

    Citra and Mosaic go in cooler, for aroma not bite.

    80 °C · 30 min
  5. 11:00

    Knock out

    Cooled and sent to fermenter four. Yeast pitched.

    19 °C
  6. Day 3

    Dry hop

    Another hop charge while the yeast is still busy.

    6 g / litre
  7. Day 7

    Crash

    Chilled hard so it drops bright.

    2 °C · 48 h
  8. Thursday

    Can

    Filled, seamed and in the fridge by 15:00.

    440 ml · best before 4 months
