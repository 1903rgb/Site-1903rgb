Where it comes from

High, cold nights. Slow cherries.

Hambela sits on the western edge of Guji, where farms climb past 2,100 metres. Around 800 smallholders deliver ripe cherry to the Wamena station, usually less than a hectare each, grown under false banana and native shade.

Cherries are sorted by hand, then dried whole on raised beds for 18 to 21 days and turned every hour in the heat of the day. That slow drying is where the jammy strawberry comes from.

We bought 20 bags (60 kg each) through our importer at a published FOB price of $4.10 per pound. When they are gone, they are gone.
