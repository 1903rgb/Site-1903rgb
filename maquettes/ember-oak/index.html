From green to cup

Ten days, logged to the second.

  1. Day 0

    Green arrives

    We buy through two importers who publish farm-gate prices. Every sack is weighed and checked for moisture before it goes on the shelf.

    Target moisture 10–12%

  2. Day 1

    Profile roast

    A 100 g sample roast tells us how dense the bean is. We set charge temperature and gas from that curve, not from habit.

    Light filter 15–17% development

  3. Day 2

    Cupping

    Three cups per lot, SCA protocol, scored blind by two of us. Anything below 84 goes back to the importer.

    8.25 g · 150 ml · 93°C

  4. Day 3–10

    Rest, then brew

    Filter coffees rest five days, espresso eight to ten. Your bag shows the roast date, so you know where it is in that window.

    Valve bags · best within 6 weeks
