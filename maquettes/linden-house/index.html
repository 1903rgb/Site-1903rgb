A stay, hour by hour

The day keeps its own pace.

Times are fixed so the house stays quiet. Ask, and most of them bend.

  1. 15:00

    Arrival

    Your room is ready from three. Bags go up, tea comes down to the drawing room. Arriving late? The porter waits until midnight.

  2. 18:30

    Supper

    Five courses in the Linden Room, Wednesday to Sunday. Guests have a table held until 19:30 without booking.

  3. 22:00

    Turn-down

    Shutters closed, a carafe of water, a square of chocolate from Frome. The house goes quiet at ten.

  4. 07:30

    Breakfast

    Until 10:30, weekends until 11:00. Eggs from Corsham, sourdough baked overnight, proper loose-leaf tea.

  5. 11:00

    Departure

    Check out by eleven. We keep your bags until evening so the city can have the afternoon.
