Your first 12 weeks

From empty bar to a real number

Week 0

Assess

Forty minutes with a coach. Movement screen, injury history, what you want from the bar.

Squat: 20 kg bar

Weeks 1–4

Groove

Technique first. Light, repeatable sets until the pattern looks the same on rep one and rep eight.

Squat: 40–50 kg

Weeks 5–8

Load

Small jumps, every week. 2.5 kg at a time, logged on your card and checked by your coach.

Squat: 55–65 kg

Weeks 9–12

Test

A deload, then test day. Three lifts, three attempts each, the whole class cheering the last one.

Squat: new 3RM
