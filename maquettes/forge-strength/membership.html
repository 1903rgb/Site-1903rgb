Questions

Before you lift

I've never lifted. Is that a problem?

No. Intro to the Bar runs four times a week with six people max. You start with a 20 kg bar, or a 15 kg or 10 kg technique bar if that suits you better.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly rolling plans, 30 days' notice by email. No exit fee, no phone call to a retention team.

Do you have changing rooms and showers?

Two changing rooms with four showers each, lockers with your own padlock, and a towel service at £1.

Can I bring my own programme?

On open gym, yes. Book a platform for 90 minutes and lift whatever you want. Ask the floor coach for a spot on anything heavy.
